How this works

Noor has recorded 15 answers about her farm. When you ask a question, a small AI model on your phone finds the answer that fits best and plays it. It never writes new answers, so it can’t make things up.

If no answer fits, your question is saved for Noor’s daughter, who replies at the weekend. Her reply becomes a new answer for the next visitor.

After your first visit, everything works without signal.

Prototype for the World Bank Small AI for Development hackathon. Noor is fictional. Swahili text is AI-translated and awaits native-speaker review; recordings are placeholders. Open the daughter’s view
